Superstore Sales and Profit Dashboard

Three-page Power BI report on discounts, profitability and year-over-year growth

August 21, 2026 Angela Iseriehen

The goal

To build a multi-page Power BI dashboard on the Sample Superstore sales dataset (2014-2017) that goes beyond headline sales figures and shows where the business makes and loses money.

The dashboard

  • Sales and Profit Dashboard: KPI cards and charts for overall performance
  • Discount Impact Analysis: how discounting affects profit
  • Executive Summary and Business Insights: the main findings and recommendations in one place

It uses nine DAX measures, a date table with time intelligence for year-over-year comparisons, several chart types, and interactive slicers.

Key findings

  • Tables is the worst-performing sub-category.
  • Orders discounted by more than 20% average a loss.
  • Sales grew strongly from 2016 to 2017.

Beyond the dashboard

I wrote a Business Insights and Recommendations report and a README, and published the Power BI file, a PDF export and the dataset in a public GitHub repository.
