HealthConnect Clinic No-Show Analysis

Python analysis of 5,000 clinic appointments to find what drives missed visits

September 12, 2026 Angela Iseriehen

The goal

To explore how a fictional clinic could use data and AI to reduce missed patient appointments. This was a multi-week project in the AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab, built around a dataset of roughly 5,000 appointments and 18 columns.

What I did

  • Data quality: checked for duplicates and missing values, and converted dates into proper datetime fields
  • Exploratory analysis: compared no-show rates across booking lead time, patient history, reminders and other fields
  • KPIs and visuals: defined five KPIs and produced five Matplotlib charts
  • Business insights: wrote five recommendations for the clinic
  • Collaboration: shared feature recommendations with a data science teammate who was building a prediction model

Key findings

  • About 48.5% of appointments were no-shows.
  • Booking lead time was the strongest single predictor, with no-show rates ranging from 27.8% to 60.5% across lead time bands.
  • Previous no-shows came second: 55.4% of patients with a history of missed visits skipped again, compared with 43.5% of those without.
  • The two effects compound. Across combinations of lead time and prior no-shows, the no-show rate ranged from 21.8% to 67.9%.
  • Missing reminder channel values were not random. They lined up with appointments where no reminder was sent, which matters for both analysis and modelling.

Working across tracks

My teammate's baseline model was a logistic regression with about 63% accuracy and a 0.68 ROC-AUC. I recommended two interaction features: lead time combined with previous no-shows, and reminder channel effectiveness by lead time band. The idea was to capture patterns that a model might treat as independent but that compound in practice.
