Logistics Operations Capstone

Four-dashboard Power BI analysis of a 14-table trucking and freight database, covering revenue, on-time delivery and fleet health

September 27, 2026 Angela Iseriehen

The goal

To analyse a 14-table relational logistics database covering trucking and freight operations from 2022 to 2024, and turn it into a four-dashboard Power BI report that a business leader could use to understand revenue, customers and fleet health.

The storyline

One finding runs through all four dashboards: the on-time delivery rate stayed flat at about 44.6% across the full 36 months. Every page connects back to that number, so the report reads as one story rather than four separate sets of charts.

Two ways of measuring revenue

I kept two revenue measures and documented the difference rather than quietly choosing one. Total Revenue counts linehaul revenue only ($262.53M), while Total Billed Revenue adds fuel surcharges and accessorial charges ($298.6M). The billed figure appears once, as a secondary stat on the Executive Overview.

The dashboards

  • Executive Overview: headline KPIs, including both revenue measures
  • Commercial: customer performance, with a customer type slicer
  • Fleet Operations: truck and driver performance, with an employment status slicer
  • A fourth page completes the set

A year slicer is synced across all pages, and a report-level filter excludes 2025 because only part of that year's data exists. The report uses a custom dark navy theme.

Key insight

About 65% of the fleet is made up of 2015 model-year trucks, which points to an ageing-fleet risk that sits alongside the flat delivery performance.
